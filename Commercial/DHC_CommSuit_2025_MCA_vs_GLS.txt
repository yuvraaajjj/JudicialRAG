--------JUDGEMENT---------

1. The petitioner has assailed order dated 13.08.2025 passed by the
learned District Judge (Commercial Court), whereby the joint application of
parties for referral of the dispute to mediation was dismissed and observing
that despite directions dated 27.03.2025, the petitioner/defendant did not file
evidence affidavit, the defendant‟s evidence was closed in commercial suit.
2. After addressing partly, learned counsel for petitioner on instructions
seeks permission to withdraw this petition with liberty to move appropriate
application before the trial court, pointing out that according to him, the
observation in the impugned order: “infacts, right to lead defendant evidence
is closed” is contrary to the record of the Local Commissioner.
3. Accordingly, the petition and the accompanying applications are
dismissed as withdrawn with liberty as sought.
GIRISH KATHPALIA, J
