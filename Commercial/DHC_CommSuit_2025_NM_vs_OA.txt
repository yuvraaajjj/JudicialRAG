--------JUDGEMENT---------

1. The petitioner assailed closure of defendant’s evidence in commercial
suit when despite four opportunities, no evidence was led by petitioner and
even cost was not paid; the learned trial court before closing the defendant’s
evidence even passed over the matter granting opportunity to the petitioner
to clear the previous costs, but the same was not done.
2. After addressing partly, learned counsel for petitioner on instructions
seeks permission to withdraw this petition.
3. Accordingly, the petition and the accompanying applications are
dismissed as withdrawn.
