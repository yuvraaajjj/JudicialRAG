--------JUDGEMENT---------

1. Petitioner is defending a commercial suit and seeks recalling of order
dated 27.08.2025 whereby the present petition was dismissed.
2. It is submitted that plaintiff is now examining one official from GST
Department and the record which such GST official would be producing
before the learned Trial Court does not form part of those documents which
the defendant had sought for by sending a notice under Order XII Rule 8 CPC
and, therefore, opportunity in this regard could not, have been said to be lost
out, as observed in para 8 of the order dated 27.08.2025.
3. The present application is disposed of with the clarification that the
observation appearing in para 8 of the order dated 27.082.205 is merely,
restricted to the documents as referred in notice under Order XII Rule 8 CPC.
4. Application stands disposed of in aforesaid terms.
MANOJ JAIN, J
