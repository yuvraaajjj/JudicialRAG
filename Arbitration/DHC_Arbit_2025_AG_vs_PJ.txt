--------JUDGEMENT---------

1. This is a petition filed under Section 29A (4) & (5) of the Arbitration
and Conciliation Act, 1996 seeking extension of the mandate of the
Arbitrtor by a period of 6 months for making and publishing of the
final Award.
2. Mr. Bajaj, learned counsel for the petitioner, states that there were
some documents which were filed by the petitioner which took some
time to collate the original documents and hence, the delay.
3. Mr. Jain, learned counsel appears on behalf of the respondent and states
that the petitioner is delaying the proceedings.
4. However, I am of the view that the parties are at the stage of evidence
and considerable time, effort and energy has been expended towards
the arbitration proceedings.This is a digitally signed order.

6. For the said reasons, the petition is allowed and the mandate of the
Arbitrator is extended by a period of 6 months from today.
7. The period from 12.11.2025 till today stands regularised.
8. Mr. Bakul Jain, learned counsel appearing on behalf of the respondent
shall file his vakalatnama within 10 days from today.
9. The petition is disposed of in aforesaid terms.
